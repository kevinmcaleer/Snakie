- **Packages and Modules look like the rest of Snakie, and say what they are.**
  The panel was the last one still wearing the old green-felt and manila-tag
  skin. It now uses the Flat design: plain surfaces, hairline borders, underline
  tabs and one green **Install** button per row. Each tab opens with a line
  saying what it is for: Packages are libraries published for MicroPython that
  you search for and install; Modules are what your code can `import`, on your
  computer, on the board and built into its firmware, followed by Snakie's own
  drivers. The storage bar is labelled "Board storage" and the longer notes are
  shorter and in plain words.
