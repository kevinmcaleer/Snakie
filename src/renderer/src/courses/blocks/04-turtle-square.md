# Draw a square

No wires at all for this one. The **turtle** is a pen that lives on the screen
and goes where you tell it.

> **repeat 4 times** — **move forward 100** — **turn right 90 degrees**

Four sides, four right-angle turns, and you are back where you started.

## Try it

Press **Run**. The **Turtle** panel opens on the right and draws it.

## Then break it on purpose

- Change **4** to **3** and the angle to **120**. What shape is that?
- Try **repeat 5**, **turn 72**. And **repeat 6**, **turn 60**.
- Notice the pattern: the angle is always 360 ÷ the number of sides. A circle is
  360 degrees of turning, shared out between the corners.
- Now try **repeat 36** with **turn 10** and a **forward** of **20**.

Each time, the Python on the right changes by exactly one number — the same
number you changed on the block.
